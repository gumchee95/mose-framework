# Strategic Finance & Risk Management

Use this skill for high-level financial planning, capital structure analysis, and audit preparation.

## Core Modules

### 1. Advanced Modeling
- **Sensitivity Analysis**: Impact of variable changes on ROI/NPV.
- **Scenario Planning**: Bull/Bear/Base cases.
- **Cash Flow Forecasting**: Direct/Indirect methods.

### 2. Capital & Tax
- **Cap Table Management**: Dilution modeling, ESOP pools.
- **Tax Optimization**: R&D tax credits, depreciation strategies.

### 3. Audit & Compliance
- **Readiness Checklist**: Documenting internal controls, variance reporting.
- **Fraud Detection**: Pattern analysis in expense reports.
